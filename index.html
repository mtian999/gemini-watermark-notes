Removing the visible Gemini watermark from an image you own

Gemini Watermark Remover by Pixel Clean targets the visible star watermark that Gemini puts on exported images, and that Gemini and Veo put on videos.

It runs locally

Images and clips are decoded, processed and exported in the browser. The server never receives them, and there is no sign-in.

Images

  • The pipeline uses embedded watermark templates and reverse alpha blending rather than an image model, which is why it is quick and works at source dimensions.
  • Drop a file, wait for the automatic match, compare with the original using the slider, then download a PNG or a ZIP.
  • Free covers images up to 16 MP and exports up to 2048 pixels on the long edge, with no upscaling.

Video

  • The tool downloads a separate model, detects the visible mark frame by frame and re-encodes an MP4.
  • MP4, WebM and MOV are accepted as containers, and the browser must support the codecs plus H.264 encoding.
  • A compatible audio track is copied over; if the audio cannot be used, the output is silent with a visible notice rather than a silent failure.

Honest limits

  • A product name alone cannot guarantee a match, because exports change and cropping or resizing alters the template.
  • The tool does not remove every logo and cannot reconstruct pixels the watermark replaced.
  • It does not touch SynthID, the invisible provenance signal. Removing a visible star does not mean the file carries no AI provenance data.
  • Uncertain results are labelled rather than presented as success.
